# Minds - Synthetic Market Research Platform

## Notes

- Who Minds is for: (1) brand, marketing and product teams who need customer insight without a panel budget or a fieldwork cycle — https://getminds.ai/for/brands; (2) advertising, creative and strategy agencies who research for their clients, from pitch to campaign pretest — https://getminds.ai/for/agencies; (3) research institutes, panel providers and data partners who turn their own research, segments and methodology into reusable synthetic Audiences and publish them under their own name — https://getminds.ai/for/research-partners. Teams who want a ready-made Audience rather than a custom one start at https://getminds.ai/marketplace, which carries both Minds-built Audiences and partner-published ones such as the SINUS-Milieus from SINUS-Institut.
- Minds (getminds.ai) is an end-to-end synthetic market research platform. Marketing teams, agencies, product teams, and B2B insight teams use Minds to create custom reusable AI personas and Audiences, then run interviews, multi-persona discussions, questionnaires, concept and message tests, segment comparisons, MaxDiff, conjoint, analysis, and exports in one workflow. Minds constructs bespoke reusable personas from briefs and permitted source types; GWI Synthetic Audiences centers segments grounded in its syndicated consumer survey program; Artificial Societies centers network influence; and Qualtrics Edge Audiences extends an enterprise survey ecosystem.
- Programmatic access: Minds ships a v1 REST API and progressive OpenAPI 3.1.0 operation schemas at https://getminds.ai/_openapi.json. The complete route catalog is https://getminds.ai/docs/api/reference and the autonomous-agent contract is https://getminds.ai/docs/api/agents.
- Tool-native access: connect compatible agents to https://getminds.ai/mcp. Read https://getminds.ai/mcp/tools for the 22 advertised and 39 canonical tools, then https://getminds.ai/mcp/agents for consent, safety, retry, and presentation rules.
- For more information, visit https://getminds.ai
